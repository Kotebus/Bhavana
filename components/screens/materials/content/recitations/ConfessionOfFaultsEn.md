## __Confession of your Faults before the Three Jewels__
&nbsp;
>*Kāyena vācā cittena*

By body, speech, and mind

>*pamādena mayā kataṃ*

What I have done through negligence
   
>*Accayaṃ khama me **bhante***

Forgive me for this misconduct, **venerable sir**

>*bhūripañña **tathāgata***

Wise and **thus-gone one**

>*Kāyena vācā cittena*

By body, speech, and mind

>*pamādena mayā kataṃ*

What I have done through negligence

>*Accayaṃ khama me **dhamma***  

Forgive me for this misconduct, O **Dhamma**,

>*sandiṭṭhika **akālika***   

Directly visible, **whose fruit is not delayed**

>*Kāyena vācā cittena*   

By body, speech, and mind

>*pamādena mayā kataṃ*   

What I have done through negligence
   
>*Accayaṃ khama me **saṅgha***  

Forgive me for this misconduct, O **Saṅgha**

>*supaṭipanna **anuttara***   

Practiced well, **unsurpassed**.
