## __Offering of Practice to the Three Jewels (after meditation)__
&nbsp;
>*Imāya dhammānudhammapaṭipattiyā **Buddhaṃ pūjemi**.*  

By this practice in accordance with the Dhamma I honor the Buddha.

>*Imāya dhammānudhammapaṭipattiyā **dhammam pūjemi**.*   

By this practice in accordance with the Dhamma I honor the Dhamma.

>*Imāya dhammānudhammapaṭipattiyā **Saṅghaṃ pūjemi**.*   

By this practice in accordance with the Dhamma I honor the Saṅgha.

>*Addhā imāya patipattiyā jāti jarā vyādhi maraṇamhā parimuccissāmi.*  

May this practice free me from birth, aging, illness, and death.

>*Idam me **punnam kamma** asavekkhaya vaham hotu*  

May this merit lead to the ending of all defilements.

>*Sabbe **dukha** pamojjatu*   

May all suffering be released.
